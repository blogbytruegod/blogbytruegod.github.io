About

Blog by True God

I built Kithunu Mithura — Bible by True God — as a free gift for a friend who wanted an easy, offline way to read the Bible every day. It grew from there into a full app, kept free and non‑commercial by choice.

Why it was built

  • Purpose

    For a friend, for study

    The app started as a personal project for a friend's daily reading, and stayed focused on that goal — reading and studying Scripture, not on growth or revenue.

  • Texts used

    Old, public‑domain editions

    The Sinhala Old Version (1938) and the English King James Version (1611) are both old enough to be in the public domain.

  • No monetization

    Free, with no ads or purchases

    There's no advertising, no in‑app purchases, and no plan to introduce either. The app is free for educational and personal use.

  • What's next

    More languages, more hymns

    German and Finnish translations, plus a Sinhala Hymns book, are planned for future updates.

What the app includes

  • Offline Bible reading
  • Text-to-Speech
  • Highlights & bookmarks
  • Personal notebook
  • Daily verse notification
  • Home screen widget
  • Offline search
  • Night mode & themes

The developer

Kithunu Mithura is developed and maintained under the name Blog by True God — one developer, building for personal and community use rather than commercial gain. No personal data is collected from anyone who uses the app.
