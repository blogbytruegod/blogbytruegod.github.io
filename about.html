About

Blog by True God

I build Kithunu Mithura, an Android Bible app made to give people an offline, distraction-free way to read Scripture, sing hymns, and keep their own notes in one place.

Why Kithunu Mithura

  • Scripture

    Sinhala & English side by side

    The Sinhala Bible (Old Version) and the English KJV live in the same app, for readers who move between both.

  • Worship

    Sinhala Hymns included

    A hymn book alongside the Bible text, so daily devotion and worship don't need separate apps.

  • Reflection

    A notebook that stays private

    Notes are written and stored only on the device — never uploaded anywhere.

  • Habit

    Daily verse, widget included

    A daily notification and a home screen widget keep a verse close, even without opening the app.

Built with

  • Kotlin
  • Android SDK
  • Room DB
  • WorkManager
  • App Widgets
  • Material Design

The developer

Kithunu Mithura is developed and maintained under the name Blog by True God. It started as a personal tool for daily Bible reading and grew into a full app once friends and church members asked to use it too.
