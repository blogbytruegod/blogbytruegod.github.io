Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Kithunu Mithura, plus fixes for the most reported issues.

Issue #1 · Text-to-Speech

Why isn't Sinhala Text‑to‑Speech working, or why does it sound wrong?

This almost always happens because the Sinhala voice hasn't been downloaded yet, or your phone is using a different TTS engine that doesn't support Sinhala well. Kithunu Mithura uses your Android phone's built‑in text‑to‑speech engine — it doesn't include its own voices — so this is a one‑time setup step on the phone itself, not something inside the app.

Fix 1 — Download the Sinhala voice data in Google TTS

Most Android phones use Google's engine by default. You need to manually tell it to download the Sinhala voice files.

  1. Open your phone's Settings.
  2. Search for or navigate to System > Languages & input > Text-to-speech output (on some phones, this is under Accessibility).
  3. Tap the Settings icon (gear) next to Preferred engine, which should be Speech Recognition & Synthesis from Google.
  4. Tap Install voice data.
  5. Scroll through the list, locate Sinhala (Sri Lanka) or si‑LK, and tap the download/arrow icon next to it.
  6. Once downloaded, go back one screen, tap Language, and change it from Use system language explicitly to Sinhala.

Source: voiceout.app

Fix 2 — Check for manufacturer engine conflicts

If you have a Samsung or Xiaomi device, your phone might be using the manufacturer's own TTS engine instead of Google's, which often has poor support for South Asian languages.

  1. Go back to the Text-to-speech output menu in your settings.
  2. Tap Preferred engine.
  3. Switch it from Samsung Text-to-Speech (or another manufacturer engine) over to Speech Recognition & Synthesis from Google.

General

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. The Sinhala Bible (OV 1938) and English Bible (KJV 1611) are both bundled with the app, so reading, searching, highlighting, and note‑taking all work fully offline. An internet connection is only needed to download the app itself and, separately, to download voice data for text‑to‑speech (see Issue #1 above).

General

Is Kithunu Mithura free?

Yes. The app has no ads, no in‑app purchases, and there's no intention to earn money from it. It was built as a free gift for a friend and shared for anyone's educational and personal use.

Privacy

Are my notes, highlights, and bookmarks private?

Yes. Everything you write or mark — notebook entries, highlights, and bookmarks — is stored only in local storage on your device. None of it is uploaded or sent anywhere. See the full Privacy Policy for details.

Roadmap

Will more languages or a hymn book be added?

Yes — German and Finnish translations are planned for future updates, along with a Sinhala Hymns book. Check the Projects page for the current feature list and what's coming soon.
