Android App — Bible & Devotional

Scripture, hymns, and a notebook for your quiet time — in one offline app.

Kithunu Mithura brings the Sinhala Bible (Old Version), the English KJV, and Sinhala Hymns together, with a built-in notebook, daily verse notifications, and a home screen widget so a verse is always one glance away.

Why this app

Built so Scripture doesn't need a signal.

Kithunu Mithura is designed to work fully offline — the Bible text, hymns, and your notes are all stored on the device. The only thing that reaches out is the daily notification reminding you to read.
