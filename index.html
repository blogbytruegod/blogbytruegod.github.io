Android App — Free & Non‑commercial

Read the Sinhala (OV) and English (KJV) Bible, completely offline.

Kithunu Mithura — also known as Bible by True God — was built as a free gift for a friend, and for anyone who wants a fast, distraction‑free way to read and study Scripture. No internet required: every word is bundled with the app. Both texts are old, public‑domain editions, and the app is made with no intention of earning money.

Why this app exists

Made for a friend — shared with anyone who wants it.

Kithunu Mithura started as a personal project built for a friend's daily Bible reading. It's kept free and non‑commercial on purpose: no ads, no in‑app purchases, and no intention of making money from it. Both Bible texts used — the 1938 Sinhala Old Version and the 1611 King James Version — are old enough to be in the public domain.
